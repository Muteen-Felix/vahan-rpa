# Reviewer instructions

1. Open `https://analytics.parivahan.gov.in/analytics/vahanpublicreport?lang=en`.
2. Open **VAHAN RPA Assistant** from the Chrome toolbar.
3. Confirm that dropdown options are loaded from the active VAHAN page.
4. Select a State, time period, vehicle filters, Y-Axis, and X-Axis.
5. Click **Fill Filters**.
6. Confirm that the selected values appear in the VAHAN report form.
7. Enter the CAPTCHA manually on VAHAN and click **Apply**.
8. If **Automatically export Excel** is enabled and results contain the Xlsx control, the extension clicks it when it becomes available.

No login or reviewer credentials are required. CAPTCHA cannot be automated and must be entered by the reviewer. Availability and response time of the third-party VAHAN website are outside the extension developer's control.
